# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive course on **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: Embodied Intelligence in the Real World**. This course covers the integration of artificial intelligence with physical systems, focusing on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems that enable robots to understand natural language commands, perceive their environment, and execute complex physical tasks.

## Course Overview

This 13-week course provides a complete education in Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics, covering both theoretical foundations and practical implementations:

- **Embodied Intelligence**: AI systems that interact with the physical world
- **Vision-Language-Action Integration**: Unified systems processing visual, linguistic, and action modalities
- **Humanoid Robotics**: Both manipulation and locomotion capabilities
- **Modern Technologies**: NVIDIA Isaac, LLMs, advanced simulation environments

## Key Concepts

### Physical AI Principles
- **Embodiment**: Intelligence emerges from interaction between agent and environment
- **Multimodality**: Integration of vision, language, and action
- **Real-time Processing**: Systems operating in real physical environments
- **Adaptation**: Learning and adaptation to new situations
- **Safety**: Reliable operation in human environments

### Technology Stack
- **ROS 2**: Robot operating system for communication and control
- **NVIDIA Isaac**: GPU-accelerated robotics development platform
- **Gazebo/Unity**: Physics simulation environments
- **OpenVLA**: Vision-Language-Action models for robotics
- **LLMs**: Large language models for planning and interaction
