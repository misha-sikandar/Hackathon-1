## Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

This comprehensive course covers the integration of **embodied intelligence** in robotic systems, focusing on the convergence of perception, action, and cognition in physical agents. The curriculum emphasizes **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** systems that enable robots to understand natural language commands, perceive their environment, and execute complex physical tasks.

### 🤖 Course Overview

Physical AI represents a paradigm shift from traditional digital AI to systems that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world. This course covers:

- **Embodied Intelligence**: AI systems with physical form that interact with the real world
- **Humanoid Robotics**: Systems that combine manipulation and locomotion capabilities
- **Vision-Language-Action Integration**: Unified systems that process visual, linguistic, and action modalities
- **Real-World Deployment**: Bridging simulation and reality for practical applications

### 🎯 Learning Objectives

By completing this course, you will:

- Understand the principles of Physical AI and embodied cognition
- Implement Vision-Language-Action systems for robotic applications
- Design and control humanoid robots with both manipulation and locomotion
- Integrate perception, planning, and control systems
- Deploy robotic systems from simulation to real-world environments
- Apply large language models for robotic task planning and execution

### 🔧 Key Technologies

The course covers state-of-the-art tools and platforms:

- **ROS 2**: Robot operating system for communication and control
- **NVIDIA Isaac**: GPU-accelerated robotics development platform
- **Gazebo/Unity**: Physics simulation environments
- **OpenVLA**: Vision-Language-Action models for robotics
- **LLMs**: Large language models for planning and interaction
- **SLAM**: Simultaneous localization and mapping systems

### 🏗️ Physical AI Principles

This course emphasizes the core principles of Physical AI:

- **Embodiment**: Intelligence emerges from the interaction between agent and environment
- **Multimodality**: Integration of vision, language, and action
- **Real-time Processing**: Systems that operate in real physical environments
- **Adaptation**: Learning and adaptation to new situations
- **Safety**: Reliable operation in human environments
