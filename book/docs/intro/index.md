# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: Embodied Intelligence in the Real World". This comprehensive 13-week curriculum bridges the gap between digital AI models and physical humanoid robots, teaching you how to design, simulate, and deploy embodied AI systems.

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI represents a paradigm shift from traditional digital AI to systems that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world. Unlike conventional AI models that operate on abstract data, Physical AI systems are embodied—they have physical form and must navigate the complexities of real-world physics, sensor noise, actuator limitations, and environmental uncertainties.

### Key Characteristics of Physical AI

- **Embodiment**: Physical form that interacts with the environment
- **Perception**: Sensing capabilities to understand the world
- **Reasoning**: Cognitive abilities to make decisions
- **Action**: Actuation mechanisms to effect change
- **Learning**: Adaptation through interaction with the environment

### Why Humanoid Robotics?

Humanoid robots represent the pinnacle of Physical AI challenges. They must integrate multiple complex subsystems:

- **Locomotion**: Walking, balancing, and navigating complex terrains
- **Manipulation**: Grasping, manipulating, and interacting with objects
- **Perception**: Understanding 3D environments and social contexts
- **Communication**: Natural interaction with humans
- **Cognition**: Higher-level reasoning and decision-making

## Course Overview

This book is organized into 13 weekly modules, each building upon the previous to create a comprehensive understanding of Physical AI systems:

1. **Foundation**: Theory of embodied intelligence and ROS 2 fundamentals
2. **Simulation**: Creating digital twins and virtual environments
3. **Perception**: Computer vision, sensor fusion, and spatial understanding
4. **Action**: Control systems, locomotion, and manipulation
5. **Integration**: Combining all systems into autonomous robots

## Learning Approach

Our pedagogical approach emphasizes:

- **Simulation-first**: Learn and test in virtual environments before physical deployment
- **Engineering-focused**: Practical implementation details over theoretical concepts
- **Progressive complexity**: Building from simple to complex systems
- **Real-world relevance**: Applications that matter in industry and research

## Prerequisites

To get the most out of this course, you should have:

- Basic programming experience (Python preferred)
- Understanding of linear algebra and calculus
- Familiarity with Linux/Ubuntu environments
- Access to a machine with RTX-enabled GPU for simulation

Don't worry if you're not an expert in all these areas—we'll provide foundational material to help you succeed.

## Technology Stack

Throughout this course, you'll work with industry-standard tools:

- **ROS 2**: The Robot Operating System for distributed robotics
- **Gazebo**: Physics-based simulation environment
- **NVIDIA Isaac**: GPU-accelerated perception and manipulation
- **Unity**: Advanced visualization and digital twin creation
- **OpenAI Whisper**: Speech recognition for conversational robots
